Part one

Character sheets

Three panels side by side on a grey studio backdrop: a head-and-shoulders close-up, the full body from the front with the head cropped out, and the full body from the back. Generate each at 16:9, then upload it as an Ingredient whenever that character appears.

Three-panel layout (reusable template)Swap the bracketed parts for any new character
Three-panel character reference sheet, side by side. Background in all three panels: seamless plain medium-grey studio backdrop, cool neutral grey (around #8E9196), smooth matte paper sweep, very subtle soft vignette slightly darker at the edges and lighter behind the subject, faint soft floor shadow at the feet, no texture, no props, no horizon line, no gradient colors. Soft diffused frontal studio lighting, even exposure, photorealistic, consistent character across all panels.

Panel 1 (left): tight close-up portrait from chest up, facing camera — [hair], [facial hair], [accessories], [expression], same grey backdrop softly out of focus.

Panel 2 (center): full-body front view, standing straight, arms relaxed at sides, head cropped out at the neck (headless, frame starts at the collarbone), showing outfit only — [top], [bottoms], [shoes], standing on the same grey seamless backdrop.

Panel 3 (right): full-body back view, same pose, head included, showing hair and accessories from behind, same outfit, same grey backdrop.

Fashion lookbook turnaround sheet, sharp focus, true-to-life colors, thin white dividers between panels, no text, no logos.

If the middle panel keeps a head: add "neck-down crop, no face visible in the center panel". If the backdrop comes out warm, add "warm tones, beige, white background, textured wall, outdoor" to the negative prompt.
